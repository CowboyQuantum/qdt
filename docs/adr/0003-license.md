# 0003 — Apache-2.0 license

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-10-09
**Step:** 0

## Context
The engine must be consumable by a company and potentially internalized later without legal friction, while remaining open.

## Decision
License the public repo under Apache-2.0.

## Consequences
- Easier: corporate consumption; explicit patent grant
- Harder: nothing material
- Must now: keep LICENSE at repo root

## Alternatives considered
- MIT — rejected: no patent grant.
- GPL/LGPL — rejected: friction for corporate internalization.
